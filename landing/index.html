The Problem

Storytelling breaks the moment it crosses a language

Every existing tool forces the same trade-off.

🚫 Translate-and-ship

  • A temple becomes "a temple" — the culture goes flat
  • Script and story state live in the same file, so a language switch means starting over
  • Hand-localizing per region means a new voice cast and QA pass every single time
  • Cost climbs in a straight line with every language you add

✓ Infinity Studio

  • Culture packs across 6 dimensions — real places, festivals, folklore, deities, names, and speech register per language
  • Plot and characters are state, not script — a language switch is a re-render, not a rewrite
  • Every render is scored for authenticity before it reaches a listener
  • One story generation, rendered fresh into any of 11 languages on demand

Why It's Different

What you won't find in a standard TTS tool

1
Characters persist.

Named characters from any generated story are saved with their personality and backstory, ready to be pulled into a brand-new setting later.

2
Stories branch, with real history.

Re-fork any saved story from a changed decision — a branch-of-a-branch is generated aware of its full lineage, not just the one step directly above it.

3
Music follows the scene, not the whole story.

Verified to correctly shift from calm → horror → action as a story moves through its beats, not one flat mood throughout.

4
Stories switch language — not just get translated.

Same plot, same characters, natural phrasing and names for that language, switchable later without losing the story's identity.

5
One story, four formats.

Narrated audio, scene-illustrated playback, comic panels, or an MP4 slideshow — all from data this service already generates, no re-authoring.

How It Works

From one line to a finished, scored story

Describe it

A dream, memory, or one-line scene — in plain English or any supported language.

AI writes & scores it

A cinematic short is generated with cultural grounding, then scored on phrasing, tone, and accuracy — weak drafts get one automatic rewrite.

Music, voice & visuals render

Scene-aware score and SFX, a fitting narrator voice per character, and optional cover art or a storyboard.

Listen, branch, or export

Play it back, fork a new path from any decision, switch its language, or export as video, comic, or choice-tree.

Built Honestly

What this deliberately doesn't claim

  • Cover video is a static image held for the full narration, muxed via ffmpeg — not AI-generated moving video.
  • Quality scoring judges the generated text; nothing here evaluates the synthesized audio's pronunciation.
  • One fixed voice per language from the base engine — true multi-voice casting needs a different, heavier backend.
  • Not sold as "undetectable as AI" — it's a synthetic voice, just a clearer, more produced-sounding one.
